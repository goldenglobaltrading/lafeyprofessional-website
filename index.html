YOUR PROFESSIONAL RITUAL

Simple steps, beautifully finished hair.

  1. 01

    Cleanse

    Apply shampoo to wet hair, massage gently and rinse thoroughly.

  2. 02

    Condition

    Apply the hair mask through lengths and ends, then rinse.

  3. 03

    Detangle

    Shake the two-phase conditioner well, spray evenly and comb through.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Apply a small amount of serum to lengths and ends for a polished finish.
